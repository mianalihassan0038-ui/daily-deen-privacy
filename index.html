Daily Deen – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Daily Deen ("the app") is an Islamic daily routine tracker published by Aurial Tech. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no internet access is required.

Data stored on your device

Your routine checklist, prayer records, Quran progress, tasbeeh counts, notes and settings (such as the city you choose for prayer times) are stored only on your own device using local app storage. This data never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently.

Location

The app does not access your device location (GPS). You choose a city from a list, and prayer times are calculated offline on your device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders for prayer times and Tahajjud on your device. No notification data is sent to any server.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Aurial Tech – please use the developer contact email shown on the app’s Google Play page.
