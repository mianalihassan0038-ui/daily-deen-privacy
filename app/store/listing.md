## Full description
Daily Deen helps you build a strong daily connection with Allah — one small step at a time.

Set your daily Islamic routine (mamoolat), tick each act of worship as you complete it, and watch your progress and streaks grow. Everything works offline and your data stays on your phone.

🌙 DAILY ROUTINE CHECKLIST
• Tahajjud, Fajr, Morning Azkar, Ayat-ul-Kursi, Surah Yaseen
• Ishraq, Chasht, Dhuhr, Asr, Durood 100x, Astaghfar 100x, Evening Azkar
• Maghrib, Isha, Witr, Surah Al-Mulk, last 2 ayat of Al-Baqarah, before-sleep Azkar
• Surah Al-Kahf reminder on Fridays
• Turn items on/off or add your own custom activities

🕌 NAMAZ TRACKER
• Accurate offline prayer times for your city (Hanafi / Standard Asr, Karachi, MWL, ISNA, Egypt, Umm al-Qura methods)
• Next-prayer countdown
• Record each prayer: with Jamaat, alone, Qaza or missed + Sunnah/Nafl
• Qaza prayer counter and 30-day prayer report

📖 QURAN TRACKER
• Daily pages target
• Khatam progress (604 pages) and 30 Para checklist
• Daily Surah checklist

📿 TASBEEH & AZKAR
• Digital tasbeeh: SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar, Astaghfirullah, Durood, La ilaha illallah
• Morning, evening and before-sleep Azkar with Arabic, translation and references
• Completing a target automatically ticks your routine

📊 PROGRESS
• Daily completion %, weekly chart, 30-day average
• Current & best streak (all 5 farz prayers)
• Most-missed activities and a daily Muhasaba note

🔔 REMINDERS
• Prayer time notifications and a Tahajjud wake-up reminder

🌐 Urdu + English • Light & Dark mode • Hijri date
🔒 No account, no ads, no data collection.
